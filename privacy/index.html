Bible Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Bible Buddy does not collect any data. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else. What the app knows, your plans and what you have read, stays on your devices and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud.

Plans and progress

The plan you follow, its schedule and the readings you have checked off are stored on your device, in a container the app shares with its widget. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

iCloud sync

With iCloud sync on (the default), the same plans and progress are stored in the private database of your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account show them too. The data is yours: Apple stores it under your account, and neither we nor anyone else can read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. Turn sync off under Settings → iCloud sync to keep everything on one device; to remove the data from iCloud, delete it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Reminder

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It never leaves it.

The assistant

The plan assistant (Pro) turns a description into a plan with the on-device model of Apple Intelligence when your device offers it, and with built-in rules otherwise. Your description is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Things you choose to share

Two features produce files from your data, and each one only leaves your device when you pick a destination in the iOS share sheet.

  • Plan sharing (a plan's Share button): a Bible Buddy plan file with the plan's days and readings. It holds no progress.
  • Diagnostics report (Settings → Send Diagnostics): a file with the app's own log lines, app and iOS version, device model, locale, free storage, the state of iCloud sync, and crash records if any. It may name the plan you follow and reading days; it holds no scripture and nothing you typed. Addresses and identifiers are redacted before the file is written. Send it to support@koebraam.com by mail, or not at all; nothing is uploaded in the background.

Subscriptions and purchases

Bible Buddy Pro is an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. Bible Buddy has no access to your payment details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional crash reporting), the change will be described here with a new effective date before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@koebraam.com, or Settings → Send Diagnostics in the app.
