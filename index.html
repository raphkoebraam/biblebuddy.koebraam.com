A reading plan that tells you what to read today, and gets you back on track when life happens.

Bible Buddy keeps your Bible reading plan: today's passages, a check per reading, your streak and a buffer for busy days. Fell behind? Catch up a few days at a time, or move the plan to today. It works with the Bible you already read, in print or in any app.

Free on iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later). No account, no ads. Your plans sync through your own iCloud.

Catch up

Behind is not broken

Missed a week? Pick how many days to read per sitting and see the date you are caught up. The readings come one book at a time, so Genesis stays with Genesis. Or move the plan so today is the next unread day. Nothing is skipped.

Plans

Pick a plan, pick your days

The whole Bible in a year with a Psalm and a Proverb every day, the Gospels in 40 days, the New Testament in 90. Start on any date, choose which weekdays you read, and if you are already mid-plan, start from the day you are on.

Pro

Build your own plan

Choose the books, the number of days and the days of the week. Add a Psalm or a Proverb every day. Or describe the plan you want, like "the New Testament in 90 days, weekdays only", and let the assistant draft it on your device. Share plans as files, import them from a spreadsheet.

Today

What to read, one tap per reading

Today's passages with their verse counts. Check them off as you go, or mark the day done. Browse any day of the plan with the calendar.

Read ahead

A buffer for busy days

Read tomorrow's passages today and see how many days ahead you are. The buffer carries you through the days without time to read.

Streak

Day after day

Your streak counts reading days, so a rest day in your schedule does not break it. Progress shows how far through the plan you are.

Widget

Today on the Home Screen

Today's readings in a widget, with a tap on each circle to mark it read. The Lock Screen widget shows what is left.

Reminders

A nudge at your hour

One daily reminder at the time you choose, as a local notification. Nothing else.

Pro

Specialized plans and your own

Chronological, M'Cheyne style, the Bible in 90 days, two tracks with grace days, and the plan builder with its assistant. Monthly, yearly with a free two weeks, or once for life.

Private by design

No account with Bible Buddy, no analytics, no ads. Your plans and progress stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, where only you can read them.
