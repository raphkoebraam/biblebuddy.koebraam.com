Bible Buddy Support

Need help or found a bug? In the app, open Settings → Send Diagnostics: it prepares a message to support@koebraam.com with the details we need (device, iOS version and the app's own log, never your readings). Add what you expected to happen and send it. Or write to support@koebraam.com directly.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bible Buddy include the Bible text?

No. Bible Buddy is the plan and the progress: it tells you what to read today and keeps track of what you have read. You read in the Bible you already have, a printed one or any Bible app. Books, chapters and verse counts follow the Protestant canon of 66 books.

What is in the free version?

The Bible in a Year plan (a reading from the Old Testament, a Psalm, a Proverb and a reading from the New Testament every day), the Gospels in 40 days, Psalms and Proverbs in a month, the New Testament in 90 days and as a chapter a day, and Old and New Testament together. Today, the widget, progress, streak, catch-up, reading ahead, the daily reminder and iCloud sync are free too.

What does Bible Buddy Pro add?

The specialized plans: chronological, M'Cheyne style with four tracks, weekdays only, the Bible in 90 days, two tracks with grace days, wisdom books with Psalms, and Paul's letters. The plan builder, where you choose the books, the days and the weekdays and can add a daily Psalm or Proverb. The assistant, which drafts a plan from a description like "the New Testament in 90 days, weekdays only" on your device. And plan import from a spreadsheet (CSV) or a Bible Buddy plan file.

I am behind. What now?

Today shows how many days behind you are and two ways out. Catch up: pick how many days to read per sitting, see the date you are caught up, and read the days in one list, one book at a time. Or move the plan to today: the first unread day becomes today's reading and the rest shift with it. Nothing is skipped either way.

Can I read ahead?

Yes. When you are on schedule, Today offers Read ahead: pick how many days to read now, today's first, and the days beyond it become your buffer. Today shows how many days ahead you are. Reading ahead keeps the schedule as it is, so a day without time to read costs nothing.

I am already in the middle of a plan. Can I start from where I am?

Yes. When you start a plan, set the day you are on. The days before it are marked as read, and the schedule counts from your start date.

How does the streak work?

The streak counts reading days in a row on which you finished the day's readings. Days you did not schedule (if your plan skips weekends, for example) do not break it.

How do I get a reminder?

Settings → Daily reminder, with the time you want. It is a local notification on your device; the app sends nothing anywhere.

How does iCloud sync work?

Your plans and progress are stored in your own iCloud account (CloudKit, private database) and appear on every device signed in to the same Apple Account. Nobody else can read them, including us. Turn it off under Settings → iCloud sync if you prefer to keep everything on one device; the change takes effect the next time the app starts.

The widget shows yesterday's readings.

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule, about once an hour. Open the app, or tap the widget, and it catches up at once.

How do I share a plan with someone?

Open a plan you built and tap Share: a Bible Buddy plan file goes through the share sheet. The other person imports it with Import on the Plans tab (part of Pro). Built-in plans are already in every copy of the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Bible Buddy Pro is managed by the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the subscriptions page. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The lifetime purchase comes back with Restore Purchases on the Pro page on any device signed in to your Apple Account.
